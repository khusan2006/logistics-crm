{% comment %} Renders a form's visible fields, boxing any the form declares as a group. A form opts in with: field_groups = [("Legend", ["field_a", "field_b"])] Fields keep their declared order; when the first field of a group comes up the whole group is drawn inside one
, and the rest are skipped in place. Forms that declare nothing render exactly as they always did. This exists because a modal can carry two of the same field.
