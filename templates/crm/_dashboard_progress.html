The label is written TWICE, both copies centred across the WHOLE track and so exactly on top of each other: the muted one under the fill, the inked one over it and clipped to the fill's width. As the fill grows the text turns colour through it, which is the only way one centred label reads at both 0% and 100%. The fill's width lives in --p on the track, because the clip is derived from it — one number, two uses, no chance of them disagreeing. Order in the DOM is load-bearing: muted, fill, inked.
