Nothing is summed across them on purpose: adding cash to granula to somebody else's unpaid invoice gives a number that describes no actual thing. Kassada is the hero because it is the figure somebody checks every morning; the rest read as rows under the one question each answers. As ten equal tiles it ranked nothing — a debt that is usually zero drew the eye as hard as the till. EVERY figure is printed in its own currency and never converted, and every one of them is a split: one line per currency, because that is how many facts it is. The till keeps a dollar heap and a so'm heap, a hamkor owed on a dollar kelishuv and a so'm one is owed two separate debts, the ombor holds so'm-kelishuv mol beside dollar-kelishuv mol, and a logist carries both kinds of money for us. A side holding nothing says so — "0 so'm" — in the muted tone, so the currency is never simply missing. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} The till is drawn as the three places it is actually held — naqd, karta, bank — and NOT as a grand total above them. The total answered "how much have we got", which is never the question at the safe: what is being checked is one heap at a time, and the sum of three heaps you cannot spend interchangeably is not a fact anybody acts on. Adding it back would also put the same money on the screen twice. `split_full` on every line, so both currencies are always drawn and an empty side reads as a zero rather than going missing — a card with no so'm on it says "0 so'm". Nothing between the label and the heaps. The explaining lines that used to sit here — "hozir qo'lda va hisobda turgan pul", "shundan mijoz avansi …" — said what the card already says by being the till: the one card everybody opens the page for does not need to introduce itself, and two lines of small grey type above the figures pushed the only numbers that matter down the card. An avans is still read where it is acted on — on the mijoz whose money it is. "Kassada" is the section's eyebrow rather than a line inside the card, the same way the dashboard captions a group of KPIs: it names what the card below IS, and inside the card the three method labels are the only headings, so the eye goes label → heap instead of label → label → heap. Each method wears the KPI head the rest of the app uses (crm/dashboard.html) — a tinted icon chip beside an uppercase label — and the three are ruled apart, because they are three separate heaps rather than one figure split for display. The colour lives on the chip alone: the money keeps the text colour, since a green naqd and a violet bank would read as good and bad news rather than as two places. {% endcomment %}
